Analysis Overview

This linear regression analysis examines the relationship between study time and academic performance among students. Each blue dot represents an individual student's data, while the red line shows the linear trend predicted by our regression model.

Key Findings

The Regression Equation: Shows how performance changes with each additional hour of study. The coefficient indicates the average performance increase per hour studied.

R² Score (Coefficient of Determination): Measures how well study hours predict performance:

  • R² ≥ 0.8: Study hours are an excellent predictor of performance
  • R² = 0.6-0.8: Study hours have a strong influence on performance
  • R² = 0.4-0.6: Study hours moderately affect performance
  • R² < 0.4: Other factors may be more important than study time

What This Means

The linear model reveals a clear pattern: more study hours generally lead to better performance. However, the scatter of points around the line shows that study time isn't the only factor - teaching quality, prior knowledge, learning methods, motivation, and other variables also play important roles.
